1. In the raid: subtle, off-center, never on your character

The bid popup sits at a screen edge. The dashed gold box is the center safe zone: nothing of ours is allowed in it, and nothing covers your character. Every frame is movable, lockable, scalable and fades with opacity.

5. Nobody bid: the salvager decides

If nobody wants an item, it's offered lowest-first. Whoever claims it (usually at the bottom, so it costs almost nothing) decides what happens. If everyone passes, it goes to the Master Looter or designated disenchanter, then the guild bank.
