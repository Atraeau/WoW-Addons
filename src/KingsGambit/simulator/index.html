King's Gambit simulator

The real loot engine, running in your browser over a simulated raid. You are the Master Looter ("You") and a raider like any other: your place on the list moves as you win, the same as everyone's. Whoever is at the bottom is the salvager.

King's Gambit is a transparent loot system for WoW: Forever, derived from Suicide Kings. This page runs the addon's own Lua code in your browser (through fengari, MIT license); nothing is sent anywhere. Source and design notes.
Item stats come from the CMaNGOS classic database (GPL-3.0); drop rates were observed on Wowhead Classic. This is a training simulation, not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
